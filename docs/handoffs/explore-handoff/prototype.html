Explore Recipes — handoff prototype

The scope of this handoff: the Explore pill on Recipes, the Explore page, one-tap Add to family, the quick view sheet, the full Recipe preview, and the empty family page with chef Gator. Variant A was picked; B and C were dropped.

Scenario

Theme

Flow

  • Recipes → Explore: the Explore pill in the header, or From Explore in the “+” menu. Explore is pushed with a Back button.
  • Explore: search and chips, then You can make this and All Recipes. Tap + on a row to add it: the + turns into ✓, a toast offers Undo, and Back shows a +n badge.
  • Quick view: tap a row. You get the facts, the ingredients, the Base Recipe's Variants (each with a +), See the whole Recipe, Add to your family's Recipes and Make a Variant.
  • Full preview: the Ingredients, Steps and About tabs. A bottom bar replaces the Dock and holds Make a Variant and Add to family.
  • Empty family: chef Gator pops in and then stirs; tap him to make him hop. Below him come Explore Recipes, New Recipe and the “Start with these” rows with a +.

The family list, the Make a Variant editor and the search fall-through are only here for context. Their designs belong to other handoffs.
