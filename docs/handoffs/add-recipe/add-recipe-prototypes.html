Add Recipe Prototypes

Add RecipeThree compact layouts for the family recipe editor (New Recipe, Make a Variant, Edit). Pick the app theme to check all three skins.

What it is today

One long stacked form: title, three number fields, three native selects, then every ingredient line as five rows (name, quantity, unit, note, optional, move/remove buttons), every step as a textarea plus a timer field plus three buttons, then the guidance fields. Six ingredients and four steps run past 3,000 pt tall. The three prototypes keep every field and rule from recipeDraft.ts, but each ingredient and step is one row, the rare fields (note, optional, reorder) open on demand, and the whole page fits in two or three screens. Example content is a draft lemon chicken soup; nothing is saved.

Interactive: type to add an ingredient (Catalog search, or “Add by name”), tap ⌄ on a row for note and optional, hold a row (or grab its grip) to drag it to a new place, add timers to steps, switch tabs, open the sheets. Save shows a toast.

AOne scroll, dense rows
  • Everything on one page in the detail-page order: title, a facts strip (Serves · Prep · Cook · Level), Cuisine and Method pickers, Ingredients card, Steps card, collapsed guidance.
  • An ingredient is one 50 pt row: grip, name, quantity + unit, ⌄. The ⌄ reveals note, optional and move/remove.
  • Adding is a search row at the bottom of the card; a typed name that matches nothing becomes “Add … by name”.
  • Steps: number, auto-growing text, a + Timer chip. Sticky Save bar; Cancel beside it.
BTabs, like the detail pageChosen
  • Same three sticky tabs as the detail page: Details · Ingredients · Steps, with counts. A viewer learns one shape for reading and editing.
  • Details holds title, facts, Cuisine/Method and guidance. Ingredients and Steps each get the full screen and the keyboard never covers another section.
  • Save sits in the header, disabled until the title is set; the tab shows a red badge when it holds a problem.
  • One form for all three entries. Edit opens on Steps and shows each step’s tips inline (a Tip chip adds one); a Variant shows copied tips as a count.
  • Hold a row (500 ms, the row tints while you hold) or grab its grip to lift it and drag it to a new place; the others slide out of the way. Move up/down stay in the ⌄ strip for keyboard and screen readers.
  • Add a step rows sit before the first and after the last step, drawn like a step with a dashed “+” circle in the number column.
COverview and sheets
  • The page is a one-screen overview: title, facts, pickers, then three summary cards (Ingredients, Steps, Guidance) that show what is filled in.
  • Tapping a card opens a bottom sheet with the same compact editor as A; Done closes it and the card updates.
  • An empty required section is outlined; Save stays enabled as long as the title is set (lines and steps are optional today, as in the API).
  • Best fit for “Make a Variant”, where most sections are already full and only one usually changes.
