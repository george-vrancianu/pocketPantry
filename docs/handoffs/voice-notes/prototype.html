The flow, step by step

  • pressRecording starts on pointer down, no hold delay, so the first word isn't clipped. Haptic 10 ms. The button grows to 1.14 and turns the recording colour; the Listening card opens next to it with a live level meter, elapsed time, the Scan Language and which list it goes to.
  • < 0.3 sA quick tap is not a Voice Note. Nothing is sent or charged; a tip says “Hold to talk, let go to send”.
  • slide off72 pt outside the button arms cancel. The card goes grey with “Let go to cancel”, haptic 6 ms. Sliding back re-arms recording. Letting go there drops the audio: toast “Cancelled. Nothing was sent.”
  • 50 sTimer turns amber with “10 s left”. The progress ring around the button fills over the full minute.
  • 60 sStops itself and sends what was recorded. Toast “Stopped at 1 minute. Sending what you said.”
  • let goReading pill: spinner, “Reading your note”, length and language, Cancel. The page stays usable underneath. Haptic 14 ms.
  • resultReview sheet slides up over the page you were on. Failed send keeps the recording with Retry / Discard; silence shows “Didn't catch anything” and isn't charged; Voice Cap names tomorrow and says Scans still work.
  • Review“What I heard” first (collapsed), then changes grouped by list, default list first. Tap a change to fix amount, unit, expiry, Location, the Batch or the Ingredient. × drops one. Not found and Didn't understand sit at the bottom, read-only.
  • ApplyOne button writes everything. Disabled while a change still needs a Batch. Sheet closes, changed rows flash on the page, toast with the count and anything skipped. No Undo (ADR 0017).

Decisions

  • 1Language chip is its own tap target beside the button, never part of the hold area. Tap opens English / Română / Dansk; the pick is remembered and shared with the camera chip. While recording it's shown read-only in the card.
  • 2Defaults are visible but quiet. On Pantry adds, a Location or expiry the app filled in carries “usual” or “default” after it, with a dotted underline, so a Member sees what they didn't say.
  • 3Discard asks once inside the sheet (“You'll have to say it again”). The scrim doesn't close Review, so a stray tap with full hands can't lose the note.
  • 4Apply waits for Batch picks (decided 18 Oct): the button stays off with “Pick a batch first” until every change that needs a Batch has one.
  • 5Accessibility: holding isn't possible with VoiceOver / TalkBack, so with a screen reader the button toggles (double-tap to start, double-tap to send, Escape or two-finger scrub to cancel). Keyboard: hold Space, Esc cancels.
  • 6Hidden, not disabled, when voice isn't configured. After a Voice Cap error the button stays but dims and shows the cap message on press instead of recording.
  • 7Only “Didn't understand”: Review still opens with the transcript expanded, Apply reads “Nothing to apply” and Discard becomes the main button.
Press and hold the voice button with the mouse or a finger, then let go. Drag away from it to cancel. Space works too when it has focus.
