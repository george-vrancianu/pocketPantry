Pantry health widgetThe to-do version from home-widgets, now collapsible. Four ways to fold it. Tap a to-do to see its items; every button changes the sample and the score.

Variant

    Shared by all four

    • 1Two levels of opening. The widget opens to the to-do list; each to-do opens to its items. One to-do open at a time.
    • 2Bulk on the row, single on the item. The row button fixes all of it (Toss 2, Add 2). Items get their own small action: Toss, Used, + add, Set date, Still good.
    • 3+n on a row is how many points fixing it all would add. Rows that can't be fixed from here (Use soon, Date) show no gain on the row, only on their items' result.
    • 4Every change has Undo in the toast (5 s), and the score animates to its new value.
    • 5Open state is remembered per device (like the Row hint), so a Member who likes it open keeps it open. When the list empties, the widget closes itself and shows “Nothing to do”.
