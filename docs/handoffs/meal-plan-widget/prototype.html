    How “not enough” is worked out

    The plan is walked in order, day by day and Breakfast → Lunch → Dinner. Each meal takes its share from the Pantry, earliest-expiring Batch first, so a later meal only sees what is left. A Batch that will have expired by that day doesn't count. Everything else follows the Recipe matching rules (ADR 0007): quantities at the planned Servings, g↔kg and ml↔l only, Pantry Basics and optional lines skipped, benefit of the doubt for uncomparable Batches.

    The Shopping List never counts as stock, but a shortfall already on the list is shown as On list (amber) instead of Short (red), so the warning goes quiet once you have done something about it. Live for the phone on the right:

    New data this needs

    Meal Plan Entry (per Family): date, slot (Breakfast · Lunch · Dinner), Recipe, Servings. The widget reads entries from today to today + 6, so “1 to 7 days” is simply how many of those dates have entries; nothing stores a plan length. Shortages are computed on the client from Batches and the plan, like Pantry grouping, and never stored.
