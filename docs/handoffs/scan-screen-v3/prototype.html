An iPhone-style camera for PocketPantry

The layout follows the iOS Camera app: a glass bar on top, a glass footer on the bottom, the feed running full-bleed behind both.

Footer

Mode names slide along the bottom, selected one centred in green. Swipe the names or the viewfinder, tap a name, or use ← →.

Shutter sits above them, in the iPhone shape. Double-tap on the guide is gone; the corners are only a framing aid now.

Photo stack on the left, where iOS shows the last photo. It replaces the queue column and Done: the badge counts the photos, amber with a spinner while any is still being read, green once all are read. Tap it to open Review.

Import on the right, where iOS has the flip-camera button. It adds photos from the phone's library to the session in the current mode; they're read in the background like camera shots. It's the way in when camera access is denied.
