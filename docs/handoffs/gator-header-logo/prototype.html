Gator in the headerThe clay Gator as the app's logo in the top bar. Tap him: he hops (the Tap animation) and the screen refreshes its data. Three ways to make room for the Home greeting.

Variant

    Options

    Tap → refresh, step by step

    0 msTap. 10 ms haptic. The Tap animation starts (squash, hop, hat flip, squint; 0.9 s, ends at rest). Every query on the screen is invalidated and refetched. 0–endFetching. The date line under the greeting (or the Pantry subtitle) becomes “Refreshing…” with a small spinner. Widgets dim to 55 % but keep their numbers, so nothing jumps. Gator blinks faster while the fetch runs longer than his hop. endDone. Widgets fade back with the new data; the line reads “Updated just now” with a check for 2.5 s, then returns to the date. Rings and counts animate to their new values. failOffline. Line reads “Couldn't refresh · showing cached data” in the soon colour; no toast. Gator still hops, the tap always gives feedback. againTap while fetching. He hops again; no second fetch is started (the one in flight continues).

    Notes

    • 1Which Gator. The happy head from the app icon (the one that winks and tips the hat on Log in), in the Soft clay finish from the gator-finish handoff. At 44 pt he is above the 32 pt line where Lit flat takes over, so the gradients and shadow stay.
    • 2Dark theme. On the dark bar the drop shadow vanishes, so the head gets the light rim (#9FD36A, 2.5 px, 35 %) instead, as the finish handoff says.
    • 3Pull to refresh stays. Tapping the logo is a second way in, and the only one that works for VoiceOver without a gesture: the button is labelled “PocketPantry · refresh”.
    • 4Health widget Gator. When Home shows a Gator in the Health widget (dapper, hmm, worried), the header one still only moves on tap, so there is one animated Gator at a time.
