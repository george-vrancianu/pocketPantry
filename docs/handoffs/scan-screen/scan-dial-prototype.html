Review

How the dial behaves

Selected = centred. Only the icon in the ring carries its name, in the accent colour above it. Neighbours are dimmed and smaller, and the strip fades at the edges so it reads as a wheel, not a toolbar.

Swipe anywhere low. A horizontal drag on the dial or the viewfinder scrolls the strip live under the finger and snaps to the nearest icon on release; a quick flick advances one step. A tap on any icon jumps straight to it. On desktop, ← → also work.

Guides follow the mode with a 400 ms morph, so the swipe has visible consequence in the frame, not only in the strip.
