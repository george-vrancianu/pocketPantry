Add and Import, split

Variant B is the approved design. A and C are kept for context. The shared Add sheet loses its tabs. Add items opens ingredient search only. Import opens a separate sheet that copies lines from another list into this one. Three ways to place the two buttons and to pick what gets imported. Everything in the phone works.
