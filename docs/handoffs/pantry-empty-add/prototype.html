Pantry: empty state and Add — handoff

The handoff scope only: the empty Pantry (Shelves) and the Add sheet (Quick sheet) with Take a photo and Scan barcode, plus the camera they open. Every button leads somewhere, and whatever you add lands in the Pantry list. The spec is handoff.md next to this file.

Theme
Scenario

Behaviour

  • Scan and Say it never get their own camera or mic. Every camera button opens the app camera (scan-screen v3) with its mode already selected: the Pantry's Scan on RECEIPT, Take a photo on PRODUCT, Scan barcode on the new BARCODE mode (between Product and Ingredients, guide 0.80 × 0.24 with a scan line, reads without the shutter). A small “Adds to your Pantry” pill shows when the camera was opened from Add; Say it lights the mic half of the Dock capsule with the intro's butter pulse and a callout, so people learn where it lives.
  • Move from your list appears only when the Shopping List has ticked items, and starts Finish Shopping (Review, then Batches with Default Expiry). In the prototype it skips the Review.
  • Gator plays the one-shot entrance, then his signature loop; tap him to hop. All motion stops under reduced motion. Gator's lines name real features only.
  • The add flows use CatalogSearch (rows, token, aisle tint) and the preset chips from amount-presets. When the Pantry is empty, “Gone from your Pantry” has nothing to show, so the empty search shows Popular to start instead.
  • Defaults always come from the Catalog: unit and amount scale, Location, and Default Expiry (Family override first). The usual choice is marked so people know where a value came from.

Open questions
