Row gesturesSwipe left to delete (ignore · ask · delete, by distance), hold to edit. No action buttons on the row.

Gestures

Swipe left · three zonesThe Delete tab is attached to the row and slides in with it over a band that fades in. Tab partly visible: let go and the row springs back, nothing happens. Tab fully in view (92 pt): the band goes solid, the icon eases up a step, the row dims, a tick. Let go → inline confirmation. Past the 2⁄3 line: the band flips to solid red, the icon turns white and grows again, "Release to delete" shows, a stronger tick. Let go → the row flies off and is removed, Undo in the toast.
Hold to editThe row settles to 99 % and a soft tinted circle grows out from under the finger over 500 ms, breathing a faint accent shadow and one slow ring onto the row as it spreads. When it covers the row, a last ring drifts out, the row eases back, the phone ticks, and the edit sheet opens. Moving more than 8 pt cancels the hold.
Confirm inlineFrom the middle zone the row turns into "Remove X?" with Keep and Remove. A thin bar counts down 6 s; when it runs out the row comes back untouched. Remove collapses the row and offers Undo for 5 s. The edit sheet keeps a Remove link as the no-gesture path.
Tap stays tapPantry: nothing yet (opens the item later). Shopping: ticks the item. Star keeps its own tap. Scrolling vertically never starts a swipe.

On a desktop drag with the mouse to swipe and hold the button to edit. On a phone the hold also fires a short vibration where the platform allows it.
